Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the typing test, your results, multiplayer races and the site itself. If your question isn't here, see the contact page.

The typing test

Is PuggieType free?

Yes. The typing test and multiplayer races are free to use, with no account and no paid tier.

Do I need an account?

No. Solo tests work as soon as the page loads, and multiplayer gives you a random nickname for the session. There are no sign-ups, logins or profiles.

When does the timer start?

With your first keypress. You can read the first words before you begin without it costing you anything.

How do I restart a test?

Press tab then enter. If you want a one-key restart, set quick restart to tab, esc or enter in the settings.

Which test should I take to measure my speed?

A 60-second time test in English, without punctuation, is a good standard measurement. 15-second tests always score higher and are better for warming up. Compare results over several tests, not one.

Why did my test say "invalid"?

The result screen marks a test invalid when it was too short (a timed test under 15 seconds or a word test under 10 words), when you stopped typing for the last five seconds, when accuracy was below 75%, or when the speed was implausibly high. You still see the result, it just isn't treated as a real one.

Are my results saved?

Your settings are saved in your browser. Individual results are not kept in a history or on a leaderboard. PuggieType only keeps the speeds of your last 10 valid 30-second English tests in your browser, to estimate your pace for Quick Match.

Speed and accuracy

What is the difference between WPM and raw?

WPM counts only words you typed completely correctly. Raw counts everything you typed, mistakes included. Both use five characters as one word. The WPM and accuracy guide has the formulas and worked examples.

Why is my accuracy below 100% when I fixed every mistake?

Accuracy counts every key you pressed at the moment you pressed it. Fixing a typo with backspace corrects the text, but the wrong keypress is still counted.

What does consistency mean?

It is a score from 0 to 100% for how steady your pace was from one second to the next. Even typing scores high; bursts and pauses score low.

What is a good typing speed?

Around 40 wpm is a typical speed for everyday computer users. 60 to 80 wpm is comfortably fast, and above 100 wpm is fast by any standard. Accuracy matters as much as speed: aim for 96% or more.

How can I get faster?

Practice a little every day, type with all ten fingers from the home row, and prioritize accuracy. Our typing tips include a 15-minute practice plan.

Multiplayer

How do I race my friends?

Go to the race page, create a room, and send the invite link or code. When everyone has joined and pressed ready, the host starts the race. Rooms hold up to eight players.

What is Quick Match?

Quick Match puts you in a 30-second English race with players near your speed. If nobody suitable is around within about 15 seconds, Pug Bots fill the race. See the multiplayer guide for how matching works.

Who are the Pug Bots?

Computer-controlled racers generated by our server. They are always named "Pug Bot · ~NN wpm" and carry a visible "Bot" label, so you always know when you are racing one.

Can I choose my own nickname?

No. The server gives you a random pug-themed name, which you can reroll. This keeps names friendly without needing moderation or chat.

Is there an age limit?

Multiplayer is for players aged 13 and over. The solo typing test has no age requirement.

What happens if someone cheats?

The server recomputes every racer's speed from what they typed and flags results that don't add up. You can also report a player from the race screen. Players flagged or reported repeatedly are matched only with bots in Quick Match.

Settings and the site

Can I turn off the pug?

Yes. Open the settings and switch off pug under appearance.

Can I type in other languages or use my own text?

Yes. Click the language name above the words to choose from hundreds of languages and word lists, or use custom mode to type your own text. The modes and settings guide covers both.

Does PuggieType work on phones and tablets?

It works in mobile browsers with a touch keyboard or an attached keyboard, but the test is designed for physical keyboards, and results on touch screens aren't comparable with keyboard results.

Do you use tracking or analytics?

No analytics or tracking scripts run on PuggieType; we only see aggregate traffic totals from our host, Cloudflare. Solo test data stays in your browser; the only exception is that joining Quick Match sends your average speed from recent solo tests and a random Quick Match ID. The privacy policy lists exactly what the multiplayer server handles and for how long.

Is PuggieType open source?

Yes, under the GPL-3.0 license. The full source code is on GitHub. Read more on the about page.

How do I report a bug?

Open an issue on GitHub, or email us as described on the contact page. Tell us your browser, device and the steps that cause the problem.
