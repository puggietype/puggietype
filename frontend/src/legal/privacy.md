# Privacy Policy

_Effective date: November 4, 2026. Last updated: November 4, 2026._

## Who we are
PuggieType is run by the PuggieType team. For any privacy question or request, email support@puggietype.com.

## Solo typing tests
The solo typing test needs no account. Your settings, themes, custom text, tags and test results are stored only in your browser (localStorage). For Quick Match, your browser also keeps a short list (key "puggie-mp-recent-pace") of the speed, test mode, length and language of your last 10 solo 30-second English tests, with no dates or text; it is only used to send your average speed when you join Quick Match. Your browser also keeps a random Quick Match ID (key "puggie-mp-client-id", in localStorage and IndexedDB). It isn't linked to your nickname or account and is sent to our server only when you join Quick Match, so we never match you against yourself (for example, from a second tab). The server holds it in memory only while you're in the queue and never logs or stores it. Apart from that average speed, this ID, and a leaderboard score you choose to submit (see Leaderboards), this data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself. Clearing your browser data deletes them. Some optional features make your browser contact a third party directly, which then receives your IP address: opening the release notes (GitHub's public API), the poetry and Wikipedia text modes (poetrydb.org and Wikipedia), and an optional easter-egg video (YouTube, which may set its own cookies).

## Multiplayer (ages 13+)
Multiplayer has three modes: **private lobbies** (join by invite link or code), **Open Rooms** (public lobbies anyone can browse and join), and **Quick Match** (we match you with players near your speed). When you use multiplayer, our game server at ws.puggietype.com processes:
- **a random nickname** that the server assigns (you can't type your own);
- **the lobby code** and lobby settings;
- **race data**: progress, speed, accuracy and results;
- **connection data**: your IP address (needed to connect you, and to apply connection and spam limits), and a random player ID and session token.

**Who sees what.** Your nickname and race data are shown to the players in your room. Anyone with a private lobby's invite link or code can join it while it is open. This includes up to 60 seconds after everyone has left. Lobbies are private unless the host chooses to list them in Open Rooms. A listed lobby is public: anyone can see its code and join it. Quick Match rooms are public in the same way. The public Open Rooms list shows each listed lobby's code, its race settings, how many players and bots are in it out of the maximum, and roughly how long ago it was opened (for example "opened < 5 min ago"). It also shows a total of players racing right now, which counts people only, never bots. It doesn't show nicknames or anything else about the players.

**Speed matching (Quick Match).** To match you with players near your speed, your browser sends the server the average speed of your last 10 solo tests in the Quick Match setup (30 seconds, English), taken from a small list kept in your browser (see above). If you have no such tests, it sends 50 wpm. During your session, the server also keeps a verified speed based on your multiplayer races. We use these only to match you with similar players, and we also use your IP address to apply Quick Match rate limits. This matching speed is not a leaderboard score. It is kept in memory for your session and never saved.

**Pug Bots.** Quick Match may include computer-controlled Pug Bots. Bots are named "Pug Bot · ~NN wpm" (for example, "Pug Bot · ~70 wpm") and always carry a visible "Bot" label. The number is the bot's approximate target pace, not its result. They never count toward stats or rankings and are generated by the server. They don't use any recorded player data, they can't be reported, and they are never counted as people in player counts.

**How long we keep it.** Except for leaderboard scores (see Leaderboards) and player reports (below), lobby and race session data is kept in the server's memory only while your session or room exists, plus a 20-second reconnect window if you drop. After the last player leaves a private lobby or Open Room, the room itself (its code and race settings, with no player data) stays open for up to 60 seconds so someone can rejoin, and then it is deleted. If a host removes a player from a room, the server keeps one-way hashes of that player's IP address and temporary session ID for 10 minutes, so they can't immediately rejoin that room. That session data is not saved to disk, a database or logs. There is no chat, and players can't send each other messages.

**Logs.** Our game server doesn't write IP addresses, nicknames, lobby codes, race data or reports to its logs. Its logs contain only startup messages, error reports, overall counts every 5 minutes (for example, how many players are connected), and a note, with no details, each time a Quick Match starts. Our hosting providers keep their own technical logs, described below.

## Leaderboards
The 'Show my score on the leaderboard' setting defaults on, but a score is posted only when you press a Submit score button (Submit score to leaderboard, or Submit score & next test, & repeat or & retry saving). We then save your randomly assigned nickname and your speed (wpm), accuracy and raw speed. Solo scores are lightly checked by our server for impossible speeds, but they aren't verified like multiplayer scores, so they appear on a separate 'Solo' board. We save these scores in our database, which is hosted for us by Neon on Amazon Web Services servers in Oregon, USA. We don't store your IP address or any other identifier with it. Daily, weekly and monthly boards reset on UTC time and are cleared when each period ends. All-time boards keep scores for up to 12 months. Each board shows only its top 100 scores, and lower scores are deleted. You can turn 'Show my score on the leaderboard' off before you press a Submit score button, or email us your nickname, board, and the approximate date and score, and we'll find and remove it. We may remove scores that look cheated or that break our rules.

Some pages, like leaderboards and results, connect to our game server to check which features are turned on.

## Player reports
You can report a player in your room for cheating, being away (AFK), a problem with a generated name, or another issue, choosing from a fixed list of reasons. You can't type any text. When a report is made, we keep:
- a report ID;
- the room code;
- the reason chosen;
- the time of the report;
- a **hashed** IP address of the reported player's connection (for IPv6, only the first 64 bits are used). This is a one-way code made with a secret key that is held only in memory, never stored, and destroyed and replaced every 7 days. Without that key it can't be turned back into the IP address, and it can't be linked across 7-day periods.

We don't keep your IP address itself, player IDs, nicknames, messages or any other content with a report. Separately, and only in memory for the rest of the reported player's session, the server keeps a one-way hash of the IP address (or, if no connection address is available, of the temporary session ID) of each player who reported them for cheating, so that it can count distinct reporters; your own session also remembers which players you reported, so you can report each one only once. These are deleted when the session ends and are never written to disk or logs. Reports are kept separately from our server logs and never written to them. We use reports only to prevent abuse, for example to spot repeat cheaters or spam. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in keeping races fair and safe (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). A hashed IP address or session ID is still personal data, so we treat it with the same care. Reports are **deleted automatically after 7 days**. Players repeatedly reported for cheating by different people, or repeatedly flagged by our automatic race checks, may be limited in Quick Match to matches with Pug Bots for the rest of their session. This limit applies to Quick Match only, isn't saved, and ends with the session.

## Steam sign-in (desktop app)
The PuggieType desktop app on Steam can let you sign in with your Steam account. Sign-in happens on Steam's own website in your browser; we never see your Steam password. When you sign in, Steam tells our game server your public Steam ID, and our server asks Steam whether your account owns PuggieType (and any supporter pack) and what your Steam display name is. We use this only to sign you in, unlock what you own, and show your display name if it passes our name filter and you haven't hidden it. Our server does not store or log your Steam ID or display name; it holds them in memory only while handling the sign-in request. We don't sell or share Steam data, or use it for marketing. Our game server runs on Fly.io; see Service providers. Steam data is provided by Valve 'as is', without warranty. Valve's own handling of your data is described in Valve's Privacy Policy (https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/).

## Accounts (ages 13+)
You can use PuggieType without an account. If you create one, we process:
- **Sign-in details:** your email address and password (stored only in hashed form by Google Firebase Authentication), or, if you sign in with Google, your Google account ID, email address and whether it's verified. We don't receive your Google password, contacts or other Google data.
- **Account record:** a random account ID, a generated pug name, your email address, whether it's verified, when you created the account, and the scores and settings you save.
- **Age check:** we ask for your birth year once, only to check that you're old enough. We don't send it to our servers or store it. Only a yes/no result is used. If the answer means you can't have an account, we delete the sign-in that was just made, and we keep only a note in your browser to stop the question being asked again.
- **Security check:** sign-in and sign-up use Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks your browser and device signals (such as your IP address) to tell people from bots. Turnstile doesn't track you across sites for advertising.

**Why (legal basis):** to provide the account you ask for (performance of a contract, GDPR Art 6(1)(b)), and to keep the service secure and stop abuse (legitimate interests, Art 6(1)(f)).

**Who processes it for us:** Google (Firebase Authentication, and Google Sign-In if you use it), Cloudflare (Turnstile), Neon (our account database, hosted in the US, Oregon), and Fly.io (our server). They act as our processors under their data processing terms.

**International transfers:** this data is stored and processed in the United States. For users in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

**How long we keep it:** for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete your account record and Firebase sign-in straight away. Database backups are overwritten within about 6 hours, and any leftover sign-in data is removed within 30 days. Cloudflare and Google keep limited security logs as described in their own privacy policies.

**Deleting your account:** sign in, open the sign-in page, and choose "delete account". You can also email support@puggietype.com and we'll do it within 30 days.

**Your rights:** depending on where you live (including under GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act), you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing support@puggietype.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We don't sell or share your personal information, as those terms are defined in California law.

**Children:** accounts are only for people aged 13 and over. If you're a parent or guardian and think a child under 13 has an account, email support@puggietype.com and we'll delete it.

## Cookies, analytics and error reporting
PuggieType doesn't load any analytics, error-reporting or advertising scripts, and doesn't set analytics or advertising cookies. The only traffic statistics we use are the aggregate totals Cloudflare produces from its own request data (see above). We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If we ever add an analytics or error-reporting script, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

## Children
Multiplayer is only for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian tells us at support@puggietype.com that a child under 13 used multiplayer, we will delete any information we hold. Most multiplayer session data is deleted when the session or room ends, player reports are deleted after 7 days, and leaderboard scores are kept only as described under Leaderboards.

## Your rights
