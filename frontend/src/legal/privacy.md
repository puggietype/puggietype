# Privacy Policy

_Effective date: October 17, 2026. Last updated: October 18, 2026._

## Who we are
PuggieType is run by the PuggieType team. For any privacy question or request, email support@puggietype.com.

## Solo typing tests
The solo typing test needs no account. Your settings, themes, custom text, tags and test results are stored only in your browser (localStorage). They never leave your device unless you export them yourself. Clearing your browser data deletes them. Some optional features make your browser contact a third party directly, which then receives your IP address: opening the release notes (GitHub's public API), the poetry and Wikipedia text modes (poetrydb.org and Wikipedia), and an optional easter-egg video (YouTube, which may set its own cookies).

## Multiplayer (private lobbies, ages 13+)
When you create or join a lobby, our game server at ws.puggietype.com processes:
- **a random nickname** that the server assigns (you can't type your own);
- **the lobby code** and lobby settings;
- **race data**: progress, speed, accuracy and results;
- **connection data**: your IP address (needed to connect you), and a random player ID and session token.

Your nickname and race data are shown only to the players in your lobby. Anyone with your lobby's invite link or code can join it while it is open. All of this is kept in the server's memory only while the lobby exists, plus a 20-second reconnect window if you drop. It is not saved to disk or a database, and it is deleted when the lobby closes. There is no chat, and players can't send each other messages.

Our game server does not write IP addresses, nicknames, lobby codes or race data to its logs. Its logs contain only startup messages, error reports and overall counts every 5 minutes (for example, how many players are connected). Our hosting providers keep their own technical logs, described below.

## Why we use this data (GDPR legal bases)
- To provide the typing test and the multiplayer races you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b), performance of a contract).
- To keep the service secure and prevent cheating and abuse (Art. 6(1)(f), legitimate interests).
- To comply with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).

## Service providers
- **Cloudflare, Inc.** hosts the website (Cloudflare Pages) and provides DNS and security.
- **Fly.io, Inc.** hosts the game server on a single server in its San Jose, California (US) region. Fly.io processes this data on our behalf as our service provider.

These providers process IP addresses and technical request data on our behalf. Fly.io keeps our game server's log output for 7 days ([Fly.io logging overview](https://fly.io/docs/monitoring/logging-overview/)). We don't receive or store raw visitor request logs from Cloudflare; Cloudflare processes and retains its own operational data as described in [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Cloudflare also asks browsers to report network errors to it (Network Error Logging), and loads a small script that protects email addresses on our pages from spam bots. If Cloudflare's bot protection is triggered, it may set strictly necessary security cookies such as `__cf_bm` or `cf_clearance`. We don't use Cloudflare Web Analytics.

## Cookies, analytics and error reporting
PuggieType doesn't load any analytics, error-reporting or advertising scripts, and doesn't set analytics or advertising cookies. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If we ever add analytics or error reporting, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

## Children
Multiplayer is only for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian tells us at support@puggietype.com that a child under 13 used multiplayer, we will delete any information we hold. Because lobby data is deleted when the lobby closes, it is usually already gone.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example under the EU or UK GDPR, or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. We do not sell or share your personal information. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. To make a request, email support@puggietype.com.

## Retention summary
| Data | How long we keep it |
|---|---|
| Solo settings and results | in your browser only, until you clear them |
| Nickname, lobby code, race data, IP address (game server) | in memory until the lobby closes, plus a 20-second reconnect window; never written to disk or logs |
| Game server logs | startup and error messages and 5-minute overall counts only; no personal data. Kept by Fly.io for 7 days |
| Cloudflare and Fly.io operational data | as described in each provider's own privacy policy |
| Emails you send us | as long as needed to answer you, then deleted |
