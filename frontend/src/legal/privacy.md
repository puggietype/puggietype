# Privacy Policy

_Effective date: October 18, 2026. Last updated: October 30, 2026._

## Who we are
PuggieType is run by the PuggieType team. For any privacy question or request, email support@puggietype.com.

## Solo typing tests
The solo typing test needs no account. Your settings, themes, custom text, tags and test results are stored only in your browser (localStorage). For Quick Match, your browser also keeps a short list (key "puggie-mp-recent-pace") of the speed, test mode, length and language of your last 10 solo 30-second English tests, with no dates or text; it is only used to send your average speed when you join Quick Match. Your browser also keeps a random Quick Match ID (key "puggie-mp-client-id", in localStorage and IndexedDB). It isn't linked to your nickname or account and is sent to our server only when you join Quick Match, so we never match you against yourself (for example, from a second tab). The server holds it in memory only while you're in the queue and never logs or stores it. Apart from that average speed and this ID, this data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself. Clearing your browser data deletes them. Some optional features make your browser contact a third party directly, which then receives your IP address: opening the release notes (GitHub's public API), the poetry and Wikipedia text modes (poetrydb.org and Wikipedia), and an optional easter-egg video (YouTube, which may set its own cookies).

## Multiplayer (ages 13+)
Multiplayer has three modes: **private lobbies** (join by invite link or code), **Open Rooms** (public lobbies anyone can browse and join), and **Quick Match** (we match you with players near your speed). When you use multiplayer, our game server at ws.puggietype.com processes:
- **a random nickname** that the server assigns (you can't type your own);
- **the lobby code** and lobby settings;
- **race data**: progress, speed, accuracy and results;
- **connection data**: your IP address (needed to connect you, and to apply connection and spam limits), and a random player ID and session token.

**Who sees what.** Your nickname and race data are shown to the players in your room. Anyone with a private lobby's invite link or code can join it while it is open. Lobbies are private unless the host chooses to list them in Open Rooms. A listed lobby is public: anyone can see its code and join it. Quick Match rooms are public in the same way. The public Open Rooms list shows each listed lobby's code, its race settings, how many players and bots are in it out of the maximum, and roughly how long ago it was opened (for example "opened < 5 min ago"). It also shows a total of players racing right now, which counts people only, never bots. It doesn't show nicknames or anything else about the players.

**Speed matching (Quick Match).** To match you with players near your speed, your browser sends the server the average speed of your last 10 solo tests in the Quick Match setup (30 seconds, English), taken from a small list kept in your browser (see above). If you have no such tests, it sends 50 wpm. During your session, the server also keeps a verified speed based on your multiplayer races. We use these only to match you with similar players, and we also use your IP address to apply Quick Match rate limits. They are kept in memory for your session and never saved.

**Pug Bots.** Quick Match may include computer-controlled Pug Bots. Bots are named "Pug Bot · ~NN wpm" (for example, "Pug Bot · ~70 wpm") and always carry a visible "Bot" label. The number is the bot's approximate target pace, not its result. They never count toward stats or rankings and are generated by the server. They don't use any recorded player data, they can't be reported, and they are never counted as people in player counts.

**How long we keep it.** Except for player reports (below), all of this is kept in the server's memory only while your session or room exists, plus a 20-second reconnect window if you drop. It isn't saved to disk or a database. There is no chat, and players can't send each other messages.

**Logs.** Our game server doesn't write IP addresses, nicknames, lobby codes, race data or reports to its logs. Its logs contain only startup messages, error reports, overall counts every 5 minutes (for example, how many players are connected), and a note, with no details, each time a Quick Match starts. Our hosting providers keep their own technical logs, described below.

## Player reports
You can report a player in your room for cheating, being away (AFK), a problem with a generated name, or another issue, choosing from a fixed list of reasons. You can't type any text. When a report is made, we keep:
- a report ID;
- the room code;
- the reason chosen;
- the time of the report;
- a **hashed** IP address of the reported player's connection (for IPv6, only the first 64 bits are used). This is a one-way code made with a secret key that is held only in memory, never stored, and destroyed and replaced every 7 days. Without that key it can't be turned back into the IP address, and it can't be linked across 7-day periods.

We don't keep your IP address itself, player IDs, nicknames, messages or any other content with a report. Separately, and only in memory for the rest of the reported player's session, the server keeps a one-way hash of the IP address (or, if no connection address is available, of the temporary session ID) of each player who reported them for cheating, so that it can count distinct reporters; your own session also remembers which players you reported, so you can report each one only once. These are deleted when the session ends and are never written to disk or logs. Reports are kept separately from our server logs and never written to them. We use reports only to prevent abuse, for example to spot repeat cheaters or spam. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in keeping races fair and safe (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). A hashed IP address or session ID is still personal data, so we treat it with the same care. Reports are **deleted automatically after 7 days**. Players repeatedly reported for cheating by different people, or repeatedly flagged by our automatic race checks, may be limited in Quick Match to matches with Pug Bots for the rest of their session. This limit applies to Quick Match only, isn't saved, and ends with the session.

## Why we use this data (GDPR legal bases)
- To provide the typing test and the multiplayer races you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b), performance of a contract).
- To keep the service secure and prevent cheating and abuse (Art. 6(1)(f), legitimate interests).
- To comply with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).

## Service providers
- **Cloudflare, Inc.** hosts the website (Cloudflare Pages) and provides DNS and security.
- **Fly.io, Inc.** hosts the game server on a single server in its San Jose, California (US) region. Fly.io processes this data on our behalf as our service provider, under a data processing agreement with us that includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

These providers process IP addresses and technical request data on our behalf. Fly.io keeps our game server's log output for 7 days ([Fly.io logging overview](https://fly.io/docs/monitoring/logging-overview/)). We don't receive or store raw visitor request logs from Cloudflare; Cloudflare processes and retains its own operational data as described in [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Cloudflare also asks browsers to report network errors to it (Network Error Logging), and loads a small script that protects email addresses on our pages from spam bots. If Cloudflare's bot protection is triggered, it may set strictly necessary security cookies such as `__cf_bm` or `cf_clearance`. Cloudflare also gives us aggregate traffic statistics (for example, total requests, visits and countries) generated from this request data at its network edge. We see only totals, never individual visitors or IP addresses, and no extra script runs in your browser for this. We don't use Cloudflare Web Analytics or any other analytics script.

## Cookies, analytics and error reporting
PuggieType doesn't load any analytics, error-reporting or advertising scripts, and doesn't set analytics or advertising cookies. The only traffic statistics we use are the aggregate totals Cloudflare produces from its own request data (see above). We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If we ever add an analytics or error-reporting script, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

## Children
Multiplayer is only for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian tells us at support@puggietype.com that a child under 13 used multiplayer, we will delete any information we hold. Most multiplayer data is deleted when the session or room ends, and player reports are deleted after 7 days, so it is usually already gone.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example under the EU or UK GDPR, or California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. We do not sell or share your personal information. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. To make a request, email support@puggietype.com.
