Contact PuggieType

The quickest way to reach us is email. Here is where to write, what to include, and what to expect after you do.

Email

Write to support@puggietype.com. One address covers everything: bug reports, multiplayer problems, reports about a player, privacy and data requests, copyright notices and general feedback. Please write in English if you can; we will do our best with other languages.

Before writing, check the FAQ. Many questions about results, settings and races are answered there, and in the guides on how WPM and accuracy are calculated and how multiplayer works.

Reporting a bug

A good bug report lets us reproduce the problem on our own machine. Please include:

  • what happened and what you expected, in a sentence or two each;
  • the steps to make it happen, starting from opening the site, for example "time mode, 60 seconds, punctuation on, then press tab during the countdown";
  • your browser and its version, your operating system, and whether you use a physical keyboard, a touch keyboard or an input method for languages such as Chinese, Japanese or Korean;
  • your keyboard layout, and whether the layout setting in PuggieType is changed;
  • the version number shown in the bottom-right corner of the site;
  • a screenshot or short screen recording, if you can.

If the problem is with your result, a screenshot of the result screen helps a lot. If it only happens with certain settings, you can export your settings from the settings page and paste them into your email.

If you are comfortable using GitHub, you can also open an issue in the public issue tracker. Issues there are public, so don't include anything private.

Multiplayer problems and player reports

To report a player during or after a race, use the report button in the room. It is faster and is tied to the right room automatically. If you email us about a race instead, include the room code, roughly when it happened (with your time zone) and what went wrong. Nicknames are random and only exist for one session, so a nickname on its own usually isn't enough for us to identify a race.

Privacy and data requests

For questions about your data or to make a request under data protection law, email the same address with "privacy" in the subject. Most of the data PuggieType handles never leaves your browser, and multiplayer data is deleted when your room closes, so in many cases there is nothing stored on our side. We will tell you clearly what, if anything, we hold. The privacy policy explains what is collected and for how long.

Copyright notices

If you believe a quote, word list or other content on PuggieType infringes your copyright, see the copyright section of our terms for the information your notice needs to include.

Security issues

If you find a security vulnerability, please email us privately rather than opening a public issue, and give us a reasonable chance to fix it before you disclose it.

Suggestions and feedback

Ideas for new modes, settings, word lists or race features are welcome. The most useful suggestions describe the problem you ran into rather than only the fix, for example "I can't tell how far ahead my friend is in a word race" instead of "add a minimap". That helps us find a solution that works for everyone. If you are suggesting a new language or word list, tell us where the words come from so we can check that we are allowed to use them.

What to expect

PuggieType is run by a small independent team, so we don't offer live support. We read every email and aim to reply within a few days; privacy and security reports are handled first. Data protection requests are answered within the time limits the law sets for them. We may not reply to every piece of general feedback individually, but we do read it all.

We will never ask for your password, payment details or any personal information beyond what is needed to answer your question.
