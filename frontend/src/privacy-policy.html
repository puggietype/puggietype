puggietype Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

puggietype is a small, open-source typing test. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Data stored in your browser

Your settings, themes and test results are stored locally in your browser (localStorage). They never leave your device unless you export them yourself. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Accounts

This build of puggietype has no accounts. We do not collect your name, email address or password.

Multiplayer races

If you join a multiplayer race, the random nickname the server assigns you and your race progress (such as typed position, speed and accuracy) are sent to the game server so other players can see them. This data is only kept for the duration of the race.

Ads and analytics

This build contains no ads and no analytics or tracking scripts.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
