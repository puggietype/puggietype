About this typing test

PuggieType is a free, minimalist typing test. Start typing and the timer begins; when it ends you see your speed in words per minute, raw speed, accuracy and consistency. The default test is 30 seconds of common English words, and you can switch to word counts, real quotes, an untimed zen mode or your own custom text, in hundreds of languages. A small pug types along with you and heats up when you go fast.

You can also race other people: create a private room and share the link with friends, or join Quick Match to race players near your speed. There is no account to create, and solo results stay in your browser. PuggieType is open source under the GPL-3.0 license.
