Race with your viewers

PuggieType is a free multiplayer typing test. If you make videos or stream, you're welcome to use it with your audience.

Make a free race room

  1. Go to puggietype.com and create a private room.
  2. Copy the room link.
  3. Paste it in your chat, description or Discord. Anyone who sees your stream or post can join, so only share links where your audience is 13+.

Rooms hold up to 8 players, and nobody needs an account to join. If more people want in, start a fresh room and post the new link.

Multiplayer races are for ages 13+.

Stream it, record it, monetize it

You don't need to ask us first. You're free to stream, record and upload videos of yourself playing PuggieType, and you can monetize those videos on your own channel. A link back to puggietype.com is appreciated but not required. This permission covers PuggieType itself. Some typing content, like quotes from books, songs or films, belongs to other people, and we can't give you rights to it.

PuggieType's code is open source under the GPL-3.0 license. That license covers the code and our current pug artwork. It doesn't give you any trademark rights in the PuggieType name, logo or pug mascot, so please don't use them as branding for your own game, app or channel. Showing them on screen while you play is fine, and you're still free to stream, record and monetize your gameplay.
